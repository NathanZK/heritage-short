The Tree That Was Always There
English narration (one paragraph per shot; the same text is in storyboard.json)

[s01] When I was about twelve or thirteen, I spent my summers in Nazreth. It's called Adama now.

[s02] I stayed with my aunt. My sister was there, and my cousin too.

[s03] In the middle of the patio in front of her house, there was a small tree.

[s04] It wasn't tall. It didn't bear fruit. It was just always there.

[s05] After breakfast, we were often sent outside while the room was cleaned.

[s06] So we climbed the tree. We took turns, and sat in its branches.

[s07] There was no game to it, really. It was a small, simple pleasure.

[s08] Then one morning, after breakfast, we went outside. The branches had been cut off.

[s09] Only the stump was left. We asked why.

[s10] I don't remember the answer, or whether anyone gave one. The three of us were sad.

[s11] It had always been there. And then it wasn't.
